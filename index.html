Oliver Wyman: HR Interview Guide

Graduate Consultant, Dubai. Built for Khaled Almuwahed.

What this round is

You passed the online assessment. Next is a video fit interview with your webcam on throughout, then 4 to 5 case interviews, each mixing a case with behavioral and CV questions. You hear after every round whether you advance. The fit interview covers your path (CV, skills, experience), role and team details, your motivation and interest in Oliver Wyman, and your questions at the end. It is a gate on motivation, communication, structure, and fit. Your stories will be reused in later rounds, so keep one consistent version of each.

What HR is scoring

  • Why consulting, why Oliver Wyman, why Dubai. Specific beats polished.
  • Structured speaking. Answer first, then support, under 2 minutes.
  • Evidence of impact. Numbers and decisions you changed.
  • Staying power. Will you stay and grow, or leave in 8 months?
  • Posting language. Initiative, intuition, creativity, an analytical mindset, and an impactful life outside your studies. Have one story for each.
  • Arabic. Listed as an advantage. Say it once, tied to clients across the Gulf region.
The thread through your story: you find where things waste time or money, fix them with data, and own the work from start to finish. Procter & Gamble is the proof: 27% lower expiry and disposal cost and 80% less reporting and approval time.

Quick formats

Stories: set the scene, say your task, say what you did, then give the result. Opinions: answer first, give a reason, then an example, then repeat the answer in one line. Why this firm: start broad with the industry, then the firm, then the role, then you.

Tell me about yourself

Say it in your own words. About three minutes at a relaxed pace, in five parts: opening, experience, consulting, SAP, and why Oliver Wyman.

Oliver Wyman is a place where I could do that across a range of clients, and I'd be really excited to grow here.

Delivery: Aim for about three minutes. If you run long, trim the 180 Degrees Consulting details first. Slow down on the 27%, say full names out loud, and stop at the end and let them take over. On video, keep the camera at eye level and look at the lens on the key numbers.

The 30-second version

Founded in New York in 1984 by former Booz Allen consultants who bet on specializing in one industry (financial services) while rivals stayed generalist. Marsh & McLennan acquired it in 2003, and it has traded as Oliver Wyman since 2007. Today the name is "Oliver Wyman, a Marsh business": the parent rebranded to Marsh in January 2026, and the consulting unit is now Marsh Management Consulting. Roughly 70+ offices worldwide.

Known for

Risk and financial services are the core. Finance and Risk is described as the largest specialized practice inside Banking & Financial Services and the cornerstone of the firm's reputation. Its work for banks includes risk, capital, liquidity, strategic planning, and financial resource management. So it is financial services and risk, not personal financial planning. The firm also covers health, transport and aviation, energy, consumer and retail, public sector, digital, and sustainability. Your posting is a general graduate role across industries, so present financial services and risk as the firm's strength, not as your assumed team. Reading one Oliver Wyman report is easy homework.
